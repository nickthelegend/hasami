鋏 hasami — scissors, and a gripper's jaws

SAM cuts it out of the frame. The arm picks it up.

Say what you want moved. Muse plans from the overhead camera once a Meta Model API key is set (wired, Setup required until then), SAM segments each object, depth turns the mask into a 3D grasp, and a simulated SO-101 arm closes its jaws on it. Every number here is SAM 2.1 with a clicked or scripted plan, not Muse.

Planner, as each JSON states it: “simulated-click oracle: the task says which object each step moves; the click is the middle of that object's colour blob in the rendered RGB image”, or “scripted Muse-format plan: written from the task by code (no language model)”, each pick hint 20 px off.

Check = physics: the arm's own check after every attempt (gripper reading + camera) agreed with MuJoCo's ground truth in 1106 of 1111 checks (1172 of 1175 before the target check; a skipped attempt never moves, so it has no check). Success is read from the physics state, never from the camera. Implausible: the located target was not something the arm could pick. It might be wider than the open jaws (41 mm), unlike any object here in size or height, outside the workspace, or a mask that ran off the frame. A boxed SAM re-prompt and a colour / raised-blob search found nothing better, so the step was skipped without moving. Under the noisy camera the arm used to fly to the table's middle (a 594 mm “target”); in the new noisy sheet no step ends aimed at something that is not an object. Mostly this relabels losses (the tipped object had already left), so the noisy sheet gains one task, 63/80 → 64/80, with 16 fewer retries. The noisy-camera sheet adds 2 mm depth noise, a 3 mm calibration error and lighting jitter; a real camera is still untested. Open problems: towers of three (12/20) and picking out of a bin (14/20). The pre-check sheets (165535Z, 170448Z, 170928Z, 171443Z) and the earlier five-family sheets (052238Z, 052444Z, 052717Z) are kept.

05 · YOUR DESK, TWINNED

One photo with an A4 sheet in it becomes a scene the arm can work in

The sheet gives the table plane and the camera's pose; SAM 2.1, prompted at a grid of points, finds every object; each mask is fitted as an upright box or cylinder whose silhouette matches it; MuJoCo builds the twin with the SO-101 beside the sheet and renders it from the photo's own viewpoint. Then the arm runs a task on the twin, checked and retried as everywhere else.

06 · APPARATUS

What runs, what waits for a key

Nothing is mocked. A missing key or device shows as Setup required, with its name.

  • SO-101 arm (simulated)Official SO-101 MuJoCo model (TheRobotStudio, Apache-2.0) with force-limited STS3215 position servos, so it sags under load and the controller corrects for it.running
  • SAM 2.1 hiera-tinyLocal, open weights, point prompts. Every number above comes from it.running
  • Muse plannermuse-spark-1.3 on the Meta Model API. Without a key the API answers setup_required, never a canned plan.Setup required: MODEL_API_KEY
  • SAM 3.1Text-prompted segmentation on the Meta Model API. Its mask decoding is unverified until a key exists.Setup required: MODEL_API_KEY
  • Check and retryAfter every attempt: the gripper's reading and the camera (pick spot clear, object at the place spot). On a miss the object is re-found and the step retried, up to twice.running
  • MCP serverlook, find, pick_place and status for an agent, over stdio.running
  • Digital twinOne desk photo with an A4 sheet becomes a MuJoCo scene; the simulated arm works in it. Measured on rendered photos only.pending · a real photo
  • Real cameraWebcam or photo, an A4 sheet for the table plane, SAM 2.1 on a clicked object. Segment and locate only; not yet checked against a ruler.pending · ruler check
  • Real SO-101 armLeRobot follower in mirror mode: it repeats the simulated arm's joint targets. Never run on hardware.Setup required: HASAMI_SO101_PORT

Margin notes

Is this a real robot?
No. It is a MuJoCo simulation of the official SO-101 model; a pick only counts if the jaws actually hold the cube.
How is success decided?
From the physics state: the cube's centre inside the tray walls and resting low. Perception is scored separately against MuJoCo's segmentation.
What works with no key?
Click an object, then a target, on the overhead camera. SAM 2.1 runs locally from that click.
